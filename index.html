Full Desmos, inside your notes.

Drop a plane anywhere on the page and type: y = 4 sin x, a parabola, a circle. Formulas and tables share one list; import CSV or draw a table by hand and Pencora reads it. Ink you write on the plane sticks to its coordinates — and the plot is drawn like pen strokes.

  • Functions, implicit curves, points, tables
  • Hand-drawn tables recognised
  • Pan and pinch inside the plane
  • One formula, linked across planes
