Pencora vs OneNote

OneNote is free and everywhere, but on iPad it needs a Microsoft account, can’t draw graphs, and turns PDFs into pictures. Pencora needs no account and puts graphs, geometry and your own handwriting on the page.

Moving over from OneNote

  1. In OneNote, send your pages to PDF (Share → Create PDF).
  2. Open the PDF with Pencora — from the Share sheet, or with Import inside a notebook — and choose to bring it in as editable ink.
  3. Pencora detects the paper ruling and turns the handwriting into real strokes, page by page. Long notebooks open at once and finish converting as you go.
