Pencora vs MyScript Notes

MyScript Notes — the app that used to be Nebo — has superb handwriting recognition, but after its trial the free version keeps notebooks to five pages. Pencora is free without limits, and adds handwriting generation, graphs and geometry.

Moving over from MyScript Notes

  1. Export the notebook as a PDF from MyScript Notes.
  2. Open the PDF with Pencora — from the Share sheet, or with Import inside a notebook — and choose to bring it in as editable ink.
  3. Pencora detects the paper ruling and turns the handwriting into real strokes, page by page. Long notebooks open at once and finish converting as you go.
