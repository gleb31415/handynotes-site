Moving over from Apple Notes

  1. In Apple Notes, export each note as a PDF (Share → Export as PDF).
  2. Open the PDF with Pencora — from the Share sheet, or with Import inside a notebook — and choose to bring it in as editable ink.
  3. Pencora detects the paper ruling and turns the handwriting into real strokes, page by page. Long notebooks open at once and finish converting as you go.
